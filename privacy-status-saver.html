Privacy Policy

Status Saver — by Niyan Tech  |  Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Status Saver, developed and published by Niyan Tech. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect (if any), how we use it, and your rights as a user.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Status Saver does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The app works entirely on your device without requiring you to create an account, log in, or share personal data with us.

3. Storage Permissions

The app requests permission to read and write to your device storage in order to:

  • View WhatsApp status files stored in the WhatsApp directory on your device.
  • Save selected statuses (photos and videos) to your gallery/downloads folder.

All file access happens locally on your device. We do not upload or access your files remotely.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data to serve personalised ads, including device identifiers and usage patterns. AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or through your device's ad settings.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
