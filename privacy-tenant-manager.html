Privacy Policy

Tenant Manager — by Niyan Tech  |  Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tenant Manager, developed and published by Niyan Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data you enter when managing your properties and tenants.

2. Data You Enter

Tenant Manager allows you to enter and manage information such as:

  • Tenant names and contact details
  • Property addresses and descriptions
  • Rent payment records and lease dates
  • Maintenance request notes

All data you enter is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to our servers, the cloud, or any third party.

3. No Account Required

Tenant Manager does not require an account or login. No personal information is sent to Niyan Tech or any external service during normal use of the app.

4. Data Security

Your tenant and property data is stored in a local database on your Android device. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device secured with a PIN or biometric lock.
  • Regularly backing up your device data.
  • Uninstalling the app and clearing app data if you sell or transfer your device.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage information to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Manage your ad preferences at Google Ad Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Tenant Manager is intended for adult property owners and managers. It is not directed at children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review it periodically.
