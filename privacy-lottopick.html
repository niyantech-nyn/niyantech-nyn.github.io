Privacy Policy

LottoPick — by Niyan Tech  |  Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LottoPick, developed and published by Niyan Tech. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you use it to generate lottery numbers and manage picks.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

LottoPick does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information to external servers. The app functions entirely offline on your device. No account, login, or personal data submission is required.

3. Locally Stored Data

To provide features like pick history and favourites, LottoPick may store data locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your device and is never shared with us or any third party.
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device and usage information to deliver relevant ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can control personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

5. Gambling Disclaimer

LottoPick is an entertainment app that generates random numbers for fun. It does not encourage, facilitate, or promote real-money gambling. Number picks are entirely random and do not guarantee any lottery outcomes.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
